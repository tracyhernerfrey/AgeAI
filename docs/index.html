Which Café Generates the Most Revenue?

Units sold tell only part of the story. To compare the cafés more meaningfully, I calculated estimated revenue using the number of units sold and the price of each item.

sales["revenue"] = sales["units_sold"] * sales["unit_price"]

cafe_revenue = sales.groupby("cafe")["revenue"].sum().reset_index()

cafe_revenue.style.hide(axis="index").format({"revenue": "${:,.2f}"})
cafe revenue
Library Cafe $3,999.75
Science Hall Cafe $2,296.00
Union Cafe $3,234.00 
cafe_revenue_sorted = cafe_revenue.sort_values("revenue", ascending=True)

fig = px.bar(
    cafe_revenue_sorted,
    x="revenue",
    y="cafe",
    orientation="h",
    title="Total Revenue by Campus Café",
    labels={
        "cafe": "Campus Café",
        "revenue": "Revenue ($)"
    },
    text="revenue"
)

fig.update_traces(
    texttemplate="$%{text:,.2f}",
    textposition="outside"
)

fig.update_layout(
    xaxis_tickprefix="$",
    xaxis_tickformat=",",
    yaxis_title=None
)

fig.show()

NoteWhat explains the difference?

Library Cafe’s revenue lead appears to be driven by sales volume. It sold 962 units, compared with 787 at Union Cafe and 551 at Science Hall Cafe, while the average unit price was $3.90 at all three locations.

What Are People Buying?

Revenue tells us which location performs best, but the item-level data can show what is driving customer demand across campus.

item_sales = (
    sales.groupby("item")["units_sold"]
    .sum()
    .sort_values(ascending=False)
    .reset_index()
)
